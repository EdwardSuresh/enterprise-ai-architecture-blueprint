| **What** | Rules for using any AI tool or system at work |

| **Where** | Applies to approved enterprise tools, embedded AI features and any public AI service |
| **Why** | Protects customers, staff, data and the organization while letting people use AI productively |

**Policy owner:** <Chief AI officer>  **Approved by:** <Executive committee>  **Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy sets the minimum rules for using AI at <Organization>.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This policy covers generative AI, AI agents, machine learning outputs and AI features in software.
2.2 It applies whether the tool is provided by <Organization> or accessed by an individual.

## 3. Approved tools

3.1 Use only AI tools listed as approved in the AI inventory or the approved tools list.
3.2 Public AI services that are not approved must not receive any data above the **Public** class (see [classification](../../data/classification.yaml)).
3.3 Requests for new tools go through the [intake form](../arb/intake-form.yaml).

## 4. Permitted use

4.1 You may use approved AI to draft, summarize, search, analyze and code, within your normal job role.
4.2 You remain responsible for any work you submit, send or act on, including AI-assisted work.

## 5. Prohibited use

5.1 Do not use AI for any purpose prohibited by law, including practices prohibited under applicable AI regulation.
5.2 Do not use AI to make a final decision that materially affects a person's rights, finances, health, employment or access to services without the human review required by the [human oversight policy](human-oversight.md).
5.3 Do not try to bypass guardrails, filters or logging.
5.4 Do not enter credentials, secrets or keys into AI prompts.
5.5 Do not use AI to impersonate a real person or to create deceptive content.
5.6 Do not use AI to monitor staff beyond what HR and legal have approved.

## 6. Data

6.1 Follow the [data use policy](data-use.md). The data class decides which model endpoints may be used.
6.2 Personal data may be used only for a purpose that is recorded in the AI inventory entry.

## 7. Accuracy and verification

7.1 Check AI output before relying on it. AI can be confidently wrong.
7.2 Do not present AI output as verified fact without checking the source.

## 8. Transparency

8.1 Tell customers and members of the public when they are interacting with an AI system, unless it is obvious.
8.2 Label AI-generated content where required by law or by internal standards.

## 9. Intellectual property

9.1 Do not enter third-party confidential material unless the license or contract allows it.
9.2 Check AI-generated code and content for licensing issues before external release.

## 10. Reporting

10.1 Report suspected AI misuse, harmful outputs or data exposure to <AI incident channel> as soon as possible.
10.2 Reports made in good faith will not lead to disciplinary action.

## 11. Breaches

11.1 Breaches are handled under existing disciplinary and information security procedures.

## 12. Review

12.1 The policy owner reviews this policy every 12 months, or sooner after a significant incident or change in law.
