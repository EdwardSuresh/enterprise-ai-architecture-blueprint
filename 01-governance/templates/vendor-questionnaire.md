# Third-Party AI Vendor Questionnaire

Send to vendors of AI products, model providers and software with AI features. Depth of follow-up depends on the risk tier of the intended use.

| 5W1H | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Sent by procurement; reviewed by security, risk, privacy and the system owner |
| **What** | Questions on the vendor's models, data handling, security, change control and evidence |
| **When** | Before contract or before enabling a new AI feature; refreshed at renewal |
| **Where** | Responses stored with the vendor record and linked from the AI inventory |
| **Why** | Third-party AI risk stays with the buyer. These answers drive contract terms and controls |
| **How** | Vendor answers every question. "Not applicable" needs a reason |
