# Control Map: ISO/IEC 42001:2023

> **Note:** Control areas are named as in Annex A. Intents are short paraphrases in our own words. ISO/IEC 42001 is a licensed standard; verify every row against your licensed copy of the standard text before audit or certification use.

| 5W1H | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Who** | AI management system owner, compliance, internal audit, certification lead |
| **What** | Kit artifacts mapped to Annex A control areas A.2–A.10 and to management-system clauses 4–10 |
| **When** | When building an AI management system (AIMS) or preparing for certification |
| **Where** | This file; reference from your Statement of Applicability |
| **Why** | ISO/IEC 42001 is the certifiable management-system standard for AI |
| **How** | Use the kit artifacts as the operating controls; your AIMS adds scope, internal audit and management review |

## Annex A control areas

| Control area | Intent (paraphrased) | Kit artifact(s) | Evidence produced |
|---|---|---|---|
| A.2 Policies related to AI | Have an AI policy, aligned with other policies, and review it | `01-governance/charter.md`, `policies/*.md` | Approved, versioned policies with review dates |
| A.3 Internal organization | Define roles, responsibilities and how concerns are reported | `raci.md`, `arb/README.md`, `policies/acceptable-use.md` (reporting) | RACI, ARB terms of reference, reports raised |
| A.4 Resources for AI systems | Know and document the data, tools, compute and people each system uses | `templates/system-card.yaml`, `data/contracts/`, `02-architecture/reference-architecture.md`, `enablement/learning-paths.md` | System cards, data contracts, skills records |
| A.5 Assessing impacts of AI systems | Assess and document effects on individuals, groups and society | `impact-assessments/template.md`, `enablement/workforce-impact-assessment.md` | Signed impact assessments |
| A.6 AI system life cycle | Manage design, development, verification, deployment, operation and retirement responsibly | `02-architecture/patterns/`, ADRs, `arb/review-checklist.md`, `ops/runbooks/`, `ops/runbooks/model-retirement.md` | ADRs, eval reports, release approvals, retirement records |
| A.7 Data for AI systems | Manage data quality, provenance and preparation | `data/classification.yaml`, `data/contracts/`, `data/lineage/`, `policies/data-use.md` | Data contracts, quality checks, lineage records |
| A.8 Information for interested parties | Give users and affected parties the information they need, and report incidents | Transparency notice in system card, `policies/acceptable-use.md` section 8, `ops/runbooks/ai-incident.md` (notify step) | Notices, user documentation, incident notifications |
| A.9 Use of AI systems | Use AI responsibly and as intended | `policies/acceptable-use.md`, `policies/human-oversight.md`, `ops/autonomy-levels.md` | Acknowledgements, oversight metrics |
| A.10 Third-party and customer relationships | Share responsibilities with suppliers and customers and manage supplier risk | `policies/third-party-ai.md`, `templates/vendor-questionnaire.md` | Questionnaires, contract clauses, vendor reviews |
